Empowering Healthier Choices with AI

NutriAI is an intelligent nutrition and meal recommendation platform designed to make healthy eating easier and more personalized.

By combining nutrition science with artificial intelligence, NutriAI helps users understand their dietary needs, track calories, and receive meal recommendations based on their personal goals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make personalized nutrition accessible to everyone. We aim to help people build healthier habits through smart recommendations, data-driven insights, and simple nutrition tracking.

Our Vision

We envision a future where technology helps individuals make better food choices and achieve their health goals with confidence.

What Makes NutriAI Different?

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered recommendations that adapt to your nutritional requirements and preferences.

🥗 Personalized Nutrition

Meal suggestions based on your profile, goals, and lifestyle.

🇳🇵 Local Food Focus

Supporting healthier choices with nutritional information for local and traditional foods.

Built With Passion
